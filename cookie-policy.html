01 What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies, such as local storage, can also remember preferences or support website functionality. Masterhaul uses cookies and similar technologies on this site.

02 How this site uses them

Masterhaul uses necessary technologies to support core website functions and to remember the cookie preferences you choose. This site also loads external resources, such as Google-hosted web fonts, which may involve limited technical requests to those providers as pages load.

03 Types of cookies we use

NecessaryRequired for the website to function correctly, including security and remembering your cookie preference. These cannot be switched off through this site's preference control.
PreferencesUsed to remember choices you make on the site, such as your saved cookie preferences, for a more consistent experience on return visits.
AnalyticsWould be used to understand website traffic and page usage in aggregate, if and when analytics services are enabled on this site.
MarketingWould be used to measure the effectiveness of advertising or marketing campaigns, if and when such services are enabled on this site.

04 Managing your cookies

You're in control of optional cookie categories. Adjust your preferences at any time on our Cookie Settings page, or block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling necessary cookies may affect how well parts of this site work.

05 Third-party cookies

Some cookies or similar technologies may be set by trusted third parties that power parts of this site, such as web-font providers. Those providers process data under their own privacy policies, and Masterhaul only works with providers that meet reasonable security and privacy standards.

06 Do Not Track

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" setting. Because there's no common industry standard for how to respond to this signal, this site does not currently respond differently based on it; you can still manage cookies directly through Cookie Settings or your browser.

07 Updates & contact

If Masterhaul adds or changes analytics, advertising, forms, or other third-party services on this site, this Cookie Policy may be updated to reflect those technologies. For questions, contact hello@masterhaul.com.

Before publication, update this page to match the site's actual analytics, advertising, consent-management, hosting, form, and third-party services.
